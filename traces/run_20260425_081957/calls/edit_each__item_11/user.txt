## test_time_compute_scaling.md
The paradigm of scaling laws in deep learning has undergone a fundamental shift from training-compute-centricity to inference-time scaling. As of 2025, the focus has moved toward "System 2" reasoning, where models allocate more computational resources during the inference phase to solve complex problems. This trend is driven by the realization that while pre-training compute provides the foundational knowledge, test-time compute allows for iterative refinement, search, and verification of reasoning paths.

Technically, this is implemented through techniques like Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS) integrated with Large Language Models (LLMs), allowing the model to explore multiple reasoning trajectories and select the most probable correct path. This approach mirrors the human ability to "think before speaking," moving away from the next-token prediction bottleneck. The integration of Chain-of-Thought (CoT) with search algorithms enables models to perform deep deliberation on mathematical, logical, and coding tasks.

The implications for hardware and software are significant. Instead of optimizing solely for high-throughput training, the industry is now investing in low-latency, high-intelligence inference engines capable of supporting massive search spaces. We are seeing a convergence where "intelligence" is no longer just a function of parameter count, but a dynamic variable determined by the computational budget allocated to a specific query. This has led to the rise of reasoning-specific architectures that can pause and compute, effectively bridging the gap between rapid pattern recognition and deep logical deduction.

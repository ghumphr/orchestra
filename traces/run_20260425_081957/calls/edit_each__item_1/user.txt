## edge_native_intelligence.md
The push for Edge-Native Intelligence is transforming how AI is deployed, moving from massive cloud-based clusters to decentralized, on-device execution. This trend is characterized by the development of models and hardware optimized for the extreme constraints of mobile, IoT, and wearable devices. The goal is to provide high-performance intelligence that is private, offline, and low-latency.

Technically, this is being achieved through radical quantization and architectural pruning. We are seeing the rise of "Sub-1-bit" quantization (e.g., BitNet), where model weights are represented using extremely low precision, drastically reducing memory bandwidth requirements. Additionally, "Hardware-Aware Neural Architecture Search" (NAS) is being used to design models that are specifically optimized for the NPU (Neural Processing Unit) architectures found in modern mobile chips.

Beyond model compression, there is a significant focus on "Federated Learning" and "On-Device Adaptation." Instead of sending user data to the cloud, models are fine-tuned locally on the device using personalized data, and only the learned weight updates (not the data itself) are aggregated. This ensures a high degree of user privacy while allowing the model to evolve and adapt to individual user behaviors. The result is a "Personal AI" that lives on your device, understands your context, and operates without constant connectivity.
